How Scout ranks upgrade opportunities
Age first. Cards issued long after a player's Hall induction, modern representatives, and obvious archive/retrospective issues rise fastest. A much older vintage card is deliberately protected from being replaced just because it has a low grade.
  • Saved older targets can raise priority because you already identified a direction for the upgrade.
  • Creases, rough condition, or low grades add pressure, but condition does not overpower age.
  • Autographs, relics, numbered/scarce cards, and rookie-designated cards get protection because a newer special card may be an intentional representative.
  • Incoming cards are skipped so Scout does not immediately recommend replacing something you just bought.
