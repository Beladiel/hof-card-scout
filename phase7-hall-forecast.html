🔮 SCOUT'S 2027 WATCHLIST

Top 5 predicted Hall candidates

This is a collecting forecast, not an election guarantee. The point is to identify cards you may want before Hall-election attention potentially changes demand.
How Scout ranks returning candidates: current vote support is the strongest input, then positive year-over-year movement, then ballot runway/urgency. A simple trend projection shows where the candidate would land if the most recent vote gain repeated. First-year candidates have no BBWAA vote history, so they must be explicitly labeled and scored separately rather than pretending votes exist.
The Hall requires 75% for BBWAA election. Players generally may remain on the ballot for up to 10 years as long as they receive at least 5%.
